# Assets and attribution

## Branding and imagery

`public/brand/v2/` contains the curved primary wordmark, stacked secondary mark, simplified small mark, icons and social artwork. The README selects forest/ivory wordmarks for light/dark backgrounds.

The six WebP interiors in `public/spaces/` are AI-generated impressions of a fictional workspace. They are not photographs or evidence of amenities or measured capacity. The catalog determines resources and seats. The provenance file records dimensions and encoding; no customer imagery is used.

Screenshots under `documentation/assets/` show the actual application with synthetic inventory in local Chrome. They contain no signed-in identity or service records. The narrow image uses mobile emulation. Generated interiors remain illustrative artwork within these screenshots.

## Workspace video

`src/components/workspace-scene.ts` builds the room, furniture, textures, foliage and camera path with Three.js and Anime.js. `scripts/export-workspace-video.cjs` renders deterministic timeline positions; `scripts/inspect-workspace-video.cjs` checks decoded frames and encoding.

The desktop/tablet/mobile files in `public/art/workspace-video/` are silent 12-second H.264 MP4s with matching WebP posters. They are build inputs, not generated by a Next.js build. The component plays them without WebGL. Regeneration requires the established browser/FFmpeg toolchain and review of resulting assets.
